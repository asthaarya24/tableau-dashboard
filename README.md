Walmart Sales Analysis & Interactive Tableau Dashboard

An interactive Tableau project analyzing Walmart weekly sales across 45 stores to understand sales trends, store performance, sales volatility, and the relationship between sales and external economic factors.
---
Project Overview
This project transforms Walmart weekly sales data into three interactive Tableau dashboards designed to answer key business questions:
- How are sales changing over time?
- Which stores are performing the best and worst?
- Which stores have the highest sales volatility?
- How do holiday periods compare with non-holiday periods?
- How does store performance vary across months?
- Are sales associated with temperature, fuel prices, CPI, or unemployment?
The final dashboards provide a combination of **KPI analysis, trend analysis, comparative analysis, heatmaps, and correlation analysis.
---
Objectives
- Analyze overall weekly sales performance
- Identify high- and low-performing stores
- Compare store-level sales and volatility
- Examine seasonal and holiday sales patterns
- Analyze monthly store performance
- Explore relationships between sales and external factors
- Present findings through interactive and easy-to-understand dashboards
---
Dashboard 1 — Sales Overview
The Sales Overview dashboard provides a high-level summary of Walmart's sales performance.
Visualizations
- Weekly Sales Trend
- Average Weekly Sales: Holiday vs Non-Holiday
- Total Sales by Store
What it shows
The dashboard highlights changes in weekly sales over time and provides an overall comparison of store sales performance.
The holiday comparison shows that average weekly sales during holidays are higher than non-holiday periods, while the store-level chart highlights substantial differences in total sales across stores.
---
Dashboard 2 — Store Performance
The Store Performance dashboard focuses on differences between individual stores.
Visualizations
- Top 10 Stores
- Bottom 10 Stores
- Store Sales Volatility
- Store × Month Heatmap
- Store Performance Matrix
Analysis
The dashboard identifies the strongest and weakest performing stores based on sales.
The Store Sales Volatility visualization compares the variation in sales across stores, while the Store × Month Heatmap provides a visual comparison of monthly sales patterns.
The Store Performance Matrix compares average weekly sales with sales volatility, helping identify stores with:
- High sales and high volatility
- High sales and lower volatility
- Lower sales and high volatility
- Lower sales and lower volatility
This provides a broader view of store performance beyond total sales alone.
---
Dashboard 3 — Sales Drivers
The Sales Drivers dashboard explores the relationship between Walmart's average weekly sales and four external factors.
Factors Analyzed
- Temperature
- Fuel Price
- Consumer Price Index (CPI)
- Unemployment
Visualizations
- Temperature vs Sales
- Fuel Price vs Sales
- CPI vs Sales
- Unemployment vs Sales
Scatter plots are used to examine how sales values are distributed across different levels of each external factor.
These visualizations show relationships and patterns in the data and should not be interpreted as proof of causation.
---
Key Findings
The dashboards provide several useful observations:
- Total sales across the analyzed stores are approximately **$6.74 billion**.
- The dataset contains **45 stores** with substantial variation in sales performance.
- Average weekly sales are approximately **$1.05 million**.
- Holiday periods show higher average weekly sales than non-holiday periods.
- Sales are concentrated among a smaller group of high-performing stores.
- Store sales volatility varies significantly, indicating differences in sales stability.
- The relationship between average weekly sales and volatility varies across stores.
- The Sales Drivers dashboard shows how sales are distributed across different temperature, fuel price, CPI, and unemployment levels.
